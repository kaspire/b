Flat stake
Ignore the 1·2·3·4 ladder — bet the base flat every time. Smoothest ride, smaller target swings.

Raindrop mode
Only bet when two neighbour columns interlock (101 + a ±1 neighbour). Rare, keeps you out of chaotic shoes. Betting engine unchanged when off.
Cluster bets
1 = bet only the first column's 101. 2 = also bet the neighbour that reached ±1. Both scored honestly (forward only).
