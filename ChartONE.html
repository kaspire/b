The line walk plots Banker as a step up, Player as a step down, a tie as a flat hold (green tick). Gold rings mark a new high (Banker breakout) or new low (Player breakout) after the silent window. The Point & Figure view rebuilds the same Banker/Player moves as columns of X (rising) and O (falling) with your chosen reversal — the cleaner way to read support and resistance. CSV exports the P&F columns.
